FUZE

Someone has hidden bombs in the tunnels under the city. You have a maze, a burning fuse and two hands to push with.

  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD. On a touch screen, swipe or use the d-pad.
  • Walk straight into a wall to push it one square. It only moves if the square behind it is free.
  • Reach a bomb before the fuse burns down. On boss levels the maze rolls in, so do not get crushed against the right edge.
